Body rig · Skinning · Procedural skirt

A personal rigging project showing Nini’s body deformation and procedural skirt. The walking breakdown transitions from skirt modules to controls and the smoothed clean character in a continuous loop.

How the skirt follows

  1. 01 Joint chains follow the hip around the skirt.
  2. 02 Leg capsules drive KeepOut contact as Nini walks.
  3. 03 The solved joints deform the skirt over the body motion.
Setup details

The module stage reveals the actual skirt setup under rig_GRP / modules_GRP / NiniDress_Module_GRP: leg collision capsules and the driven skirt chains. The deformation chains are under skel_GRP / NiniDress_Chains_GRP.

The skirt uses MayaMuscle KeepOut and matrix-driven joints. The stages share matching cameras and timing at 24 fps, with Smooth Mesh Preview enabled at level 2. The module stage uses X-ray display to reveal the mechanism inside the smoothed character.

My contribution: rigging, skinning and the procedural skirt setup. Source body motion: Adobe Mixamo, loaded from Nini’s Animation Library. The Run & fall clip uses BONES-SEED source motion (source license (opens in a new tab)).
