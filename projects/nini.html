Setup details

The module stage reveals the actual skirt setup under rig_GRP / modules_GRP / NiniDress_Module_GRP: leg collision capsules and the driven skirt chains. The deformation chains are under skel_GRP / NiniDress_Chains_GRP.

The skirt uses MayaMuscle KeepOut and matrix-driven joints. The stages share matching cameras and timing at 24 fps, with Smooth Mesh Preview enabled at level 2. The module stage uses X-ray display to reveal the mechanism inside the smoothed character.

My contribution: rigging, skinning and the procedural skirt setup. Source body motion: Adobe Mixamo and BONES-SEED, credited per clip and loaded from Nini’s Animation Library (BONES-SEED license (opens in a new tab)).
