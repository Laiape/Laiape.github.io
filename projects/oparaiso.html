Thaiz is the first character built with my autorig. I created the body and facial rig using a custom matrix-based setup, and authored the skinning.

Animation details

Each body-only study transitions through controls and clean views. Camera, motion timing and frame rate match across all stages at 24 fps.

Clothing and footwear are excluded from these tests. The body and facial rig retain their original skinning. These clips show body FK motion, without facial or finger animation.
